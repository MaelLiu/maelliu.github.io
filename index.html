STATISTICAL ARBITRAGE · OCT–DEC 2025

Crypto leveraged tokens versus perpetual futures

Motive

I noticed that since 2026, many leveraged ETF tokens have been issued on top CEXs, so I became curious about whether their repricing patterns could be traded against perpetuals.

Method

Aligned leveraged-token and perpetual/index data, measured beta decay and lag structure, then froze entry delay, exits, and costs.

Result

Although the structured and repeated pattern was discovered and characterized, retail-tier execution costs were too high to turn it into a practical strategy.

POKER SOLVER · JAN–JUN 2026

Counterfactual regret minimization poker solver

Motive

I believe the best alpha is to chase where the dumb money is, so I wanted to test whether a mathematically grounded strategy could beat weaker human players.

Method

Used external-sampling Monte Carlo CFR, card isomorphism, and a Rust regret-accumulation core for 2–4-player all-in-or-fold Hold’em.

Result

Built and deployed a solver strategy; the CV records more than 200,000 hands at a sustained 3 bb/100 result.

03

INTEREST-RATE SWAP · JUN–OCT 2026

Interest-rate swap with delta hedging

Motive

A high fixed yield caught my attention, so I wanted to see whether the exposure could be hedged rather than accepted blindly.

Method

Linked a fixed-rate leg with perpetual and leveraged-token hedges, then reconciled carry, funding, settlement, fees, and gas flat-to-flat.

Result

The completed journey earned +0.93% over 12.73 days, equivalent to about +26.66% simple annualized APR after hedging costs.
