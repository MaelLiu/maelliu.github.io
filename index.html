STATISTICAL ARBITRAGE · OCT–DEC 2025

Crypto leveraged tokens versus perpetual futures

Motive

I noticed that since 2026, many leveraged ETF tokens have been issued on top CEXs, so I became curious about whether their repricing patterns could be traded against perpetuals.

Method

Aligned leveraged-token and perpetual/index data, measured beta decay and lag structure, then froze entry delay, exits, and costs.

Result

Although the structured and repeated pattern was discovered and characterized, retail-tier execution costs were too high to turn it into a practical strategy.

POKER SOLVER · JAN–JUN 2026

Counterfactual regret minimization poker solver

Motive

I believe the best alpha is to chase where the dumb money is, so I wanted to test whether a mathematically grounded strategy could beat weaker human players.

Method

Used external-sampling Monte Carlo CFR, card isomorphism, and a Rust regret-accumulation core for 2–4-player all-in-or-fold Hold’em.

Result

Built and deployed a solver strategy; the CV records more than 200,000 hands at a sustained 3 bb/100 result.

03

INTEREST-RATE SWAP · JUN–OCT 2026

Interest-rate swap with delta hedging

Motive

A high fixed yield caught my attention, so I wanted to see whether the exposure could be hedged rather than accepted blindly.

Method

Linked a fixed-rate leg with perpetual and leveraged-token hedges, then reconciled carry, funding, settlement, fees, and gas flat-to-flat.

Result

The completed journey earned +1.86% over 12.73 days, or about 50% simple annualized net APR on 300 USDT of deployed capital. Separately, the BZ3S commodity-tracking backtest estimates roughly 10–15% annualized tracking drag.

WORLD VIEW · POWER · TECHNOLOGY

William Rees-Mogg

The Sovereign Individual with James Dale Davidson

I admire Rees-Mogg for treating technology as a force that rearranges power, then asking what an individual should do before the shift becomes obvious. The book makes me look for the mechanism underneath a political or market surface, and to build the capability to lead through a transition rather than wait for institutions to define my path.

Principle I carry: understand how the next technology changes power, then make yourself capable of acting inside that new system.

EDGE · TRANSFER · QUANTITATIVE INVESTING

Edward O. Thorp

Beat the Dealer · Beat the Market · A Man for All Markets

I admire the progression from a small, observable edge in games to a high-dimensional investing practice. His example connects closely with my own path: model the game, test the edge, control risk, and carry the method into a harder market with better data and deeper structure.

Principle I carry: find a small edge, test it rigorously, size it with discipline, and transfer the method to a larger domain.
